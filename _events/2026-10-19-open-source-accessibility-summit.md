CAOS Founder Josh Miele will facilitate open discussions about critical gaps and opportunities in open-source accessibility at GitHub's Open Source Accessibility Summit, a free, full-day event co-located with the All Things Open 2026 conference.

The summit is free, and includes lunch and the Monday evening social. [Register on the All Things Open site](https://2026.allthingsopen.org/register).
