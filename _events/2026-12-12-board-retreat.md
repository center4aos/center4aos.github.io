CAOS Board Members and officers will gather for two days of training, planning, and discussion about the future of CAOS.

This event is not open to the public, but the weekend will include a [public conference call]({{ "/events/2026-12-13-board-retreat-public-share-out/" | relative_url }}) on Sunday, December 13, to report progress and allow for public queries and comments about CAOS plans.
